About

I build the parts of a product people feel before they notice them.

I'm Kian — a product designer and creative developer working at the edge of design and engineering. For the past seven years I've helped founders, product teams, and the occasional agency turn ambitious ideas into interfaces that feel inevitable.

My process leans heavily on prototyping in code, not just static screens — which means the motion, the pacing, and the small physical details are part of the design from day one, not bolted on at the end.
